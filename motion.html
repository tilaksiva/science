Stage 2 · Discover

Rest, Motion & Reference Points

An object is in motion if its position keeps changing compared to something else. It's at rest if its position stays the same compared to something else. That "something else" is called a reference point.

We also measure distance (how far something travels) and time (how long it takes) to describe motion. Speed tells us how quickly distance is covered.

Motion isn't just moving in a straight line, either — things can move in circles, spin in place, or swing back and forth. Let's spot the reference point first!
