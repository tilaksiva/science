Stage 2 · Discover

Who Eats Whom?

Plants are called producers — they make their own food using sunlight. Animals that eat plants or other animals are called consumers. A food chain shows how energy from the sun passes from one living thing to the next.

A grasshopper is a herbivore (eats only plants). A frog and snake are carnivores (eat other animals). Some animals, like humans, are omnivores — they eat both! Ready to build your own food chain?
