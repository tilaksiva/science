Oxy's Oxygen Express 🫁

Meet Oxy, a friendly oxygen bubble! Ride along as we travel from the air, through your nose, into your lungs, and all the way to your blood.

Stage 1 · A Mystery

Hold Your Breath!

Try this: take a deep breath and hold it for as long as you comfortably can. Notice how quickly your body demands you breathe again?

Hold... 0s

Why do you think your body forces you to breathe?

Every part of your body — your brain, muscles, even your heart — needs a constant supply of oxygen to work. Let's find out how it gets there!
Stage 2 · Discover

The Journey Begins

Air enters through your nose (which warms and filters it), travels down your windpipe (trachea), and branches into your two lungs — spongy, balloon-like organs in your chest.

Inside each lung are millions of tiny sacs called alveoli — that's where I hop out of the air and into your blood! Want to try working the lungs yourself?
Stage 3 · Try It

Be the Diaphragm!

Below your lungs is a muscle called the diaphragm. Tap "Inhale" to pull it down and let air rush in, then "Exhale" to push it up and push air out.

Tap Inhale — watch the diaphragm move down and the lungs fill up like balloons!
Stage 4 · Sort It

In or Out?

When you breathe in, your body takes in oxygen-rich air. When you breathe out, your body releases air with more carbon dioxide (a waste gas your cells don't need). Sort each one!

Tap a card, then tap the correct lung.

Stage 5 · Match It

Match the Organ to Its Job

Every part of your breathing system — together called the respiratory system — has a special job. Flip the cards to match organ with job.
